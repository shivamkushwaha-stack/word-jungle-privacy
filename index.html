Word Jungle Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Word Jungle is designed to work without creating an account. The current version does not sell personal information, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or include a live third-party advertising SDK.

Information stored by the game

Word Jungle stores gameplay information on your device so that your progress is available when you return. This may include a randomly generated player identifier, an explorer name chosen by you, completed levels, stars, virtual coins and diamonds, booster inventory, daily-reward status, and music or sound settings.

This information is used only to provide game features. It is stored locally in the app's device storage and is not used to identify you in the real world.

Information we do not collect

The current version does not request or collect your email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photographs, camera or microphone recordings, payment-card details, or government identifiers. The game does not require sign-in.

Advertising and third-party services

The current version does not include a live third-party advertising SDK. Any demonstration advertising screens in the game are placeholders, do not display external advertisements, and do not transmit advertising identifiers or personal data.

If advertising, analytics, cloud saving, or another third-party service is added in a future version, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Children's privacy

Word Jungle is suitable for a broad audience, including families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no account is required, children should not enter a real full name as their explorer name. A parent or guardian may remove locally stored game information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Data sharing and sale

APX Studio does not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through the current version of Word Jungle. The current version does not collect personal information for targeted advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on the device until the user clears the app's storage, resets the device, or uninstalls the game. Since the current version does not create an online account or upload this data to APX Studio, APX Studio does not hold a server-side copy to delete.

Security

We limit data use to what is needed for the game's features. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, but keeping current gameplay data locally reduces the amount of information transmitted to external systems.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Word Jungle's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

For privacy questions about Word Jungle, contact APX Studio at apxstudiobusiness@gmail.com.
